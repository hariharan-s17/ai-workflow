03 / Why local approval matters

Local knowledge is part of the safety check.

A timetable cannot always describe what is happening on the ground. Station staff can verify operational details that affect whether a work window is safe and practical.

↔Engine changes and locomotive movements
⇄Shunting and yard activity
▤Coach attachment and movement
◎Worker protection and local conditions
“

AI can calculate a window.
People decide if it is safe to use.

The Station Master contributes current local operating knowledge. Control and divisional officers provide their required review under the applicable process.
